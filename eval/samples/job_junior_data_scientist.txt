Junior Data Scientist

We are hiring a Junior Data Scientist to build predictive models and share insights with business teams.

Requirements:
- Proficiency in Python and data libraries such as Pandas and NumPy
- Hands-on experience with machine learning algorithms using scikit-learn
- Solid understanding of statistics and hypothesis testing
- Ability to write SQL queries to extract data
- Experience communicating findings with data visualization tools such as Matplotlib or Tableau

Nice to have:
- Experience with deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow or PyTorch)
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS
- Knowledge of MLOps or model deployment
- Experience with A/B testing
